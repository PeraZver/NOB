# Operation Albia (12 August – 2 September 1942)

Operation Albia (Croatian: *Operacija Albia*) was an Axis anti-partisan offensive conducted between 12 August and 2 September 1942 in the Biokovo mountain region of occupied Croatia. The operation targeted Yugoslav Partisan forces—primarily the "Jozo Jurčević" Battalion—active in the Dalmatian hinterland between Makarska and Vrgorac. It was one of a series of Italian-led sweeps aimed at eliminating Partisan resistance along the Dalmatian coast and its inland approaches.

## Background

By the summer of 1942, Partisan units in the Makarska area had gained a foothold on the steep slopes of Biokovo, threatening Italian supply lines and the coastal garrison towns. Italian high command, under General Mario Roatta (commander, "Slovenia-Dalmatia"), ordered a concentric operation to encircle and destroy these forces before they could consolidate their position or link up with larger Partisan formations in the interior.

## Course of the Operation

The Italian 6th Corps launched a concentric advance from the coastal lowlands and the Neretva valley, with Chetnik units sealing inland escape routes. On **18 August 1942**, the main Partisan unit—the "Jozo Jurčević" Battalion—was surrounded and destroyed west of Kokorići by troops of the Bergamo Division, effectively ending organized resistance in the Biokovo area.

During and after the military sweep, Chetnik units, with Italian acquiescence, carried out mass killings of civilians and burned villages in the Makarska hinterland. Seventeen villages—including Rašćane, Kozica, Dragljane, and Župa—were razed. Between 141 and 160 civilians were killed in the initial phase; overall Croatian civilian deaths attributable to the operation reached an estimated 900. Three Catholic priests were tortured and killed. These atrocities were later cited at post-war war crimes proceedings.

## Outcome

**Axis victory.** The Italians and their Chetnik auxiliaries succeeded in temporarily eliminating the main Partisan presence on Biokovo. However, the widespread destruction and mass killings radicalized the local population, ultimately strengthening Partisan recruitment and support in Dalmatia in the following months.

| Side | Outcome |
| :--- | :--- |
| Partisan "Jozo Jurčević" Battalion | Destroyed (encircled and defeated, 18 August 1942) |
| Civilian casualties | ~900 Croatians killed by Chetnik forces; 17 villages burned |
| Strategic result | Temporary Axis control of the Biokovo region |
